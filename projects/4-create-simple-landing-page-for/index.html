WHO WE ARE

Quality assurance without the bottleneck.

TestCo was founded in 2018 by a team of QA engineers tired of watching preventable bugs reach production. Today we work as an embedded testing partner for startups and mid-size product teams across web, mobile, and API platforms.

We don't just file bug reports — we build test strategies, automation frameworks, and CI-integrated pipelines so quality becomes part of how your team ships, not an afterthought.
