The real cost of "just a quick question"

Say a customer-facing staff member spends even 90 minutes a day answering repeat questions on WhatsApp, phone, or email. That's not a guess - most SME owners I talk to know the number is close, often higher. Over a five-day week, that's 7.5 hours. Over a month, it's close to a full extra work-week, spent entirely on things a system could handle.

That time isn't just "lost." It's time not spent chasing a late payment, not spent following up a lead, not spent on the floor helping a customer with something only a person can solve. The cost isn't the 90 minutes. It's what didn't happen because of the 90 minutes.

And there's a second cost that's easy to miss: response speed. A customer who messages at 7pm and hears back at 9am the next day has usually already messaged your competitor. You don't lose that sale loudly. You just never hear from them again.
