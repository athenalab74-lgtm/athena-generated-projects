Here's a question worth asking yourself honestly: how many times this week did someone on your team answer the exact same question?

"Where's my delivery?"
"Are you open Saturday?"
"Can I get a quote for a 2018 Civic?"

If you run a workshop, a logistics company, a retail shop, or any small business with 5 to 50 people, you already know the answer. It's not once or twice. It's dozens of times a day, across WhatsApp, phone calls, and email, and it's usually the same handful of staff answering them over and over.

Here's the part most owners don't stop to calculate: what does that actually cost you?

Say one staff member spends two hours a day on repetitive questions - delivery status, opening hours, basic pricing, appointment reminders. That's not two hours of selling, fixing, or serving. That's two hours gone to typing the same answer with different words. Multiply that across a week, a month, a year, and you're looking at a meaningful chunk of a full-time salary spent on work that doesn't need a human doing it at all.

And the cost isn't just time. It's the customer who messages at 9pm and doesn't hear back until 10am. It's the lead who gets a slow reply and books with a competitor instead. It's the staff member who's mentally checked out because half their day is copy-paste admin instead of the job they were actually hired to do. Small, repeated frictions like these don't show up on a P&L line item, but they quietly cap how fast a business can grow.
